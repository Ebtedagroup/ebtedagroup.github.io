How we work

Four steps from your first message to the final file.

    Send your information

    You send us your goal, audience and business details, such as your logo, product photos and text.

  2. Step 2

    Script and first draft

    We prepare the script and a first draft and finalise them with you before production starts.

  3. Step 3

    Production

    We make the video with motion graphics or AI animation.

  4. Step 4

    Delivery

    You receive the final file sized for where it will be published: stories, reels, your website or a trade show.
